How I work

The thinking comes before the graphic.

Proof + punch

Every post pairs an emotional hook with a credible source: a joke backed by price data, a worker's quote backed by the news story. People share what moves them, but only when it won't embarrass them.

Measure what the mission measures

Followers and likes aren't always the goal. At Progress Iowa success meant supporter shares, link clicks and earned reach, so that's what I designed and reported for.

Split the job between image and caption

The graphic shows what happened in a glance. The caption explains why it matters and who it affects, so the post still lands with someone who missed the news.

Native to every platform

A portrait version for Instagram and TikTok, a landscape version for X, Facebook, LinkedIn, Bluesky and Threads, and caption lengths that fit each feed.

About

A sociologist who runs the feed.

I studied Sociology and Anthropology at Spelman College, so I came to social media already asking why people share what they share. That question shapes every post I make: who it's for, what they already believe, and what would make them pass it on.

I've run social for a national internship program, a student organization, and a statewide advocacy group. In each role I was handed something I'd never done before, learned it quickly, and built a repeatable system so the work kept going without me.
